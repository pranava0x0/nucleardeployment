Project Overview
The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) issued construction permits for the Hermes 2 test reactor facility to Kairos Power LLC on November 21, 2024 ( ML24324A021 and ML24324A022 ). Kairos Power LLC, is a U.S. based company developing a fluoride salt cooled high temperature reactor (KP-FHR) using TRISO fuel in pebble form.
Plant Characteristic
Description
Reactor Type
Two low-power test reactors to support development of Kairos Power’s fluoride salt-cooled, high-temperature reactor (KP-FHR) technology
Containment Type
Functional containment implemented principally by the high temperature TRISO particle fuel. The fuel utilizes a carbon matrix coated fuel particle, similar to that developed for high temperature gas-cooled reactors, in a pebble-based fuel element. (See SECY-18-0096 for a description of functional containment.)
Power Level
35 MW th each unit
Fuel
Tri-structural Isotropic (TRISO) fuel particles embedded in a carbon matrix pebble. High Assay Low Enriched Uranium (HALEU)
Balance of Plant
Shared steam powered conversion system (Rankine Cycle)

All public documents related to the Hermes 2 construction permit application review after acceptance may be found with a selection of Docket Numbers are equal to 05000611 and 0500612.
