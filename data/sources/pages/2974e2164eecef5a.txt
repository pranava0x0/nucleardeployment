CX-271015: Valar Ward250 Research Reactor
Valar Atomics, Inc. proposes to construct and operate the Ward250 research reactor at the Utah San Rafael Energy Research Center (USREL).
Office of NEPA Policy and Compliance
April 22, 2026
Estimated Read Time min
Valar Atomics, Inc. proposes to construct and operate the Ward250 research reactor at the Utah San Rafael Energy Research Center (USREL). Ward250 is a 250 kilowatt thermal (kWth) high temperature gas-cooled reactor (HTGR) research facility and will utilize tristructural-isotropic (TRISO) coated particle fuel, helium coolant, and graphite moderator and reflector. The facility will operate under U.S. Department of Energy (DOE) authorization. The facility will be located at USREL, a state-operated energy research facility on a 20.6-acre site in a rural area of central Utah. The Ward250 facility comprises the Reactor Complex and Fuel Laboratory enclosed within a security-fenced area occupying approximately two acres.
