Tennessee Valley Authority; Clinch River Nuclear Site, Unit 1; Notice of Hearing
The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is providing public notice of an uncontested hearing on a construction permit application from Tennessee Valley Authority for construction of Clinch River Nuclear Unit 1 at the Clinch River Nuclear Site in Roane County, Tennessee.
Document number: 2026-13662
Published: 2026-07-07
Type: Notice
Nuclear Regulatory Commission
Construction permit application; notice of uncontested hearing.
The hearing is scheduled to begin at 5:30 p.m. on August 13, 2026, at the Pollard Technology Conference Center auditorium located at 210 Badger Ave., Oak Ridge, TN 37830. This meeting will be preceded by an open house beginning at 5 p.m. at the same location.
