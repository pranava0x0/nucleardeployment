US DOE Approves PDSA For Last Energy Pilot Nuclear Reactor At Texas University
By David Dalton
29 May 2026
PWR-5 will act as ‘direct bridge’ to PWR-20 commercial unit
Construction progress on Last Energy’s PWR-5 pilot at the Texas A&M–Rellis Campus. Courtesy Last Energy.
US-based advanced nuclear technology company Last Energy announced that the US Department of Energy (DOE) has approved the company’s preliminary documented safety analysis (PDSA) for its PWR-5 pilot reactor at Texas A&M-Rellis university.
