Environmental Impact Statement
Project Overview
The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is currently reviewing the Duke Energy's Early Site Permit (ESP) application for the Belews Creek Site in Stokes County, North Carolina.
Additional information on pre-application activities can be obtained from ADAMS using a search by docket number 99902121 . Additional information on application activities can be obtained from ADAMS using a search by docket number 05200055 .
Federal Register Notices related to this ESP application are available on the federal rulemaking website by searching for docket ID NRC-2025-2161.
