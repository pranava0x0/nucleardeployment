Project Overview
The U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) is currently engaged in pre-application activities with Texas A&M University System regarding a future Early Site Permit (ESP) that would be at the RELLIS Campus in Bryan, TX. The site would house multiple advanced reactors rated between 10-1000MWe gross electrical output.
Additional information on pre-application activities can be obtained from ADAMS using a search by docket number 99902136 .
To subscribe to documents related to this project, visit GovDelivery.
