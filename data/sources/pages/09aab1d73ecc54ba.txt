NuScale Power Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Previous
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
NuScale progressed from development to physical production, signing an agreement with Doosan Enerbility for the fabrication of the most complex and foundational component of the NuScale Power Module™ (“NPM”), the upper reactor coolant system.
NuScale advanced its supply chain maturity through two additional milestones: deploying nuclear-specific artificial intelligence tools, developed with Nuclearn and NPX , across engineering and knowledge-management functions, and, together with MillenniTEK , fabricating first-of-a-kind reactor safety components for the NPMs.
NuScale continues to support its commercial partner, ENTRA1 Energy (“ENTRA1”), in their dialogue with the Tennessee Valley Authority (“TVA”) on the development of the largest commercial nuclear power deployment program in U.S. history.
NuScale maintained a strong liquidity position with $1.9 billion in cash, cash equivalents and short- and long-term investments to support immediate commercial readiness.
CORVALLIS, Ore. – November 5, 2026 – NuScale Power Corporation (NYSE: SMR) (“NuScale”, “NuScale Power” or the “Company”), the industry-leading provider of proprietary and innovative advanced SMR nuclear technology, today announced results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“This was a significant quarter for NuScale as we progressed from development to physical production of our first commercial NuScale Power Module,” said John Hopkins, NuScale President and Chief Executive Officer. “Our contract with Doosan Enerbility to fabricate the upper reactor coolant system of the NPM moves our Nuclear Regulatory Commission-approved design into actual build and sets the foundation for all modules to follow. We also continued to deepen our global supply chain and ended the period with strong liquidity, giving us the flexibility to make the strategic investments needed to move toward full-scale production.”
Liquidity & Capital Resources
Ended the third quarter of 2026 with cash, cash equivalents, and short- and long-term investments of $1.9 billion.
Financial Update
Revenue and cost of sales decreased by $8.0 million and $5.4 million, respectively, during the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to the same period in the prior year, primarily due to the revenue recognized in the prior year quarter from the work associated with Fluor’s Front-End Engineering and Design (“FEED”) Phase 2 engineering services in support of the RoPower project, which was completed in late 2025, with no comparable activity in 2026.
Research and development (“R&D”) expenses increased $7.0 million during the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025, primarily as a result of $6.7 million higher costs associated with the Company’s increased activities to advance the technological readiness and design maturity of our NPM components.
General and administrative (“G&A”) expenses decreased by $491.3 million during the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025.
Other expenses increased by $8.1 million during the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025, primarily due to (a) the Company’s engineers and project personnel working on fewer commercial projects than in the prior year, resulting in the lower allocation to Cost of sales described above and (b) higher other compensation costs incurred as we have ramped up the resources supporting supply chain readiness and the delivery of future commercial projects.
Investment income increased by $13.4 million during the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025, primarily as a result of the Company’s stronger cash position, higher interest rates and greater investments in cash equivalents, short-term investments and long-term investments.

About NuScale Power
Founded in 2007, NuScale Power Corporation (NYSE: SMR) is the industry-leading provider of proprietary and innovative advanced small modular reactor (SMR) nuclear technology, with a mission to help power the global energy transition by delivering safe, scalable, and reliable carbon-free energy. The NuScale Power Module™, the Company’s groundbreaking SMR technology, is a small, safe, pressurized water reactor that can each generate 77 megawatts of electricity (MWe) or 250 megawatts thermal (gross), and can be scaled to meet customer needs through an array of flexible configurations up to 924 MWe (12 modules) of output.
As the first and only SMR to have its designs certified by the U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission, NuScale is well-positioned to serve diverse customers across the world by supplying nuclear energy for electrical generation, data centers, district heating, desalination, commercial-scale hydrogen production, and other process heat applications.
