Scope note: this pack covers the grid-scale/large-SMR company set — NuScale Power, GE Vernova Hitachi Nuclear Energy (BWRX-300), Holtec International (SMR-300), Westinghouse (AP300 + eVinci), Terrestrial Energy. "Binding" means an executed contract, order, or PPA specifically for the company's new SMR/microreactor capacity. "Non-binding" means a framework, MOU, LOI, collaborative/planning agreement, or a self-developed project still in NRC licensing with no disclosed third-party offtake contract for that new capacity — construction-permit progress is licensing maturity, not a commercial order, and the two are not collapsed here. Non-US context (Romania, Canada, UK, Poland, Nordics, Southeast Asia) is labeled explicitly and excluded from the U.S. pipeline math.

## Terrestrial Energy

- Design: Integral Molten Salt Reactor (IMSR), a Generation IV thermal-spectrum, graphite-moderated, molten-fluoride-salt reactor using standard-assay low-enriched uranium fuel. One IMSR Core-unit is rated ~440 MWth / up to 195 MWe. The commercial IMSR400 plant pairs two Core-units for 390 MWe net. <https://www.powermag.com/terrestrial-energy-launches-390-mw-molten-salt-nuclear-reactor-design/> [press]
- Design: IMSR site footprint is small — about 7 hectares (17 acres) with a ~130m x 145m security perimeter; each Core-unit has a 7-year design life and is replaced as a sealed unit. <https://www.nrc.gov/docs/ML2009/ML20097B839.pdf> [gov]
- Funding: Nasdaq: IMSR. Went public via SPAC merger with HCM II Acquisition Corp; began trading 2025-10-29; gross proceeds exceeded $292M before expenses (including a $50M common-stock PIPE). <https://www.barchart.com/story/news/35743041/terrestrial-energy-inc-completes-business-combination-with-hcm-ii-acquisition-corp> [press]
- Funding: market cap ~$570.99M as of 2026-08-04 close ($5.39/share). <https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/imsr/market-cap/> [press]
