## Hyperscalers and data-center operators

- Microsoft signed a 20-year PPA with Constellation for the Crane Clean Energy
  Center (Three Mile Island Unit 1 restart), about 835 MW, announced
  2024-09-20. [company]
  <https://www.constellationenergy.com/news/2024/Constellation-to-Launch-Crane-Clean-Energy-Center-Restoring-Jobs-and-Carbon-Free-Power-to-The-Grid.html>
- Meta signed a 20-year PPA with Constellation for the Clinton Clean Energy
  Center in Illinois, 1,121 MW, announced 2025-06-03, deliveries from June
  2027, supporting relicensing plus a 30 MW uprate. [company]
  <https://www.constellationenergy.com/news/2025/constellation-meta-sign-20-year-deal-for-clean-reliable-nuclear-energy-in-illinois.html>
- Google and NextEra Energy announced the Duane Arnold restart 2025-10-28:
  a 25-year Google PPA on Iowa's only nuclear plant (615 MW), online target
  Q1 2029; Central Iowa Power Cooperative buys the remainder on the same
  terms. [company]
  <https://newsroom.nexteraenergy.com/NextEra-Energy-and-Google-Announce-New-Collaboration-to-Accelerate-Nuclear-Energy-Deployment-in-the-U-S?l=12>
- Amazon's Climate Pledge Fund anchored X-energy's ~$500M Series C-1,
  announced 2024-10-16, funding Xe-100 design completion, licensing, and the
  first TRISO-X fuel facility phase, with a joint target of more than 5 GW
  online in the U.S. by 2039. [company]
  <https://x-energy.com/media/news-releases/amazon-invests-in-x-energy-to-support-advanced-small-modular-nuclear-reactors-and-expand-carbon-free-power>

  disclosed. [press]
  <https://www.nucnet.org/news/amazon-buys-stake-in-nuclear-reactor-developer-in-bid-to-power-data-centres-10-4-2024>
- Oracle chairman Larry Ellison told the September 2024 earnings call the
  company is designing a gigawatt-scale data center to be powered by three
  small modular reactors, saying building permits were already secured; no
  vendor, site, or contract has been disclosed since. [press]
  <https://www.cnbc.com/2024/09/10/oracle-is-designing-a-data-center-that-would-be-powered-by-three-small-nuclear-reactors.html>

## Frontier AI labs

- Sam Altman stepped down as Oklo chairman 2025-04-22, a role held since
  2015, explicitly so Oklo could negotiate supply deals with OpenAI and other
  AI companies without a conflict; Altman had taken Oklo public through his
  AltC SPAC in May 2024. Jacob DeWitte became chairman. [press]
  <https://www.cnbc.com/2025/04/22/sam-altman-steps-down-as-oklo-chair-freeing-nuclear-company-up-to-work-with-more-ai-companies.html>
- No frontier lab has an executed nuclear offtake of its own on record as of
  2026-08-17; lab compute sits inside hyperscaler and neocloud facilities,
  so lab demand reaches reactors through the landlords (research finding
  from the absence of any executed record, not a sourced claim).

## Oil and gas

- Liberty Energy invested $10M in Oklo in 2023; CEO Chris Wright sat on
  Oklo's board and was nominated Secretary of Energy in November 2024.
  [press]
  <https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/18/trumps-pro-fracking-energy-secretary-pick-has-also-invested-in-geothermal-and-nuclear-startups>
- Alberta committed C$7M to a Cenovus-led C$26.7M feasibility study of SMRs
  for oil-sands steam (steam-assisted gravity drainage), announced
  September 2023; the Pathways Alliance (Canadian Natural, Cenovus,
  ConocoPhillips Canada, Imperial, MEG, Suncor) has evaluated SMRs for
  oil-sands decarbonization. No oil-sands SMR project has advanced to
  development. [press]
  <https://www.world-nuclear-news.org/Articles/Alberta-funds-SMR-deployment-study>
- ExxonMobil is designing a roughly 1.5 GW gas-fired plant with carbon
  capture to serve data centers, per CEO Darren Woods; rival supply to
  nuclear, not nuclear demand. [press]
  <https://www.powermag.com/exxonmobil-planning-large-gas-fired-plant-to-serve-data-centers/>
- Chevron partnered with Engine No. 1 and GE Vernova to develop up to 4 GW
  of gas plants co-located with data centers, with seven turbines ordered
  for 2026 delivery; same rival-supply posture. [press]
  <https://jpt.spe.org/chevron-partners-with-ge-vernova-and-engine-no-1-to-power-up-ai-data-centers-in-us>
- Already tracked: Oklo-Diamondback 50 MW letter of intent for Permian
  operations.

## Federal and defense

- DOE selected four federal sites in December 2025 to host AI data centers
  paired with new energy projects, including co-located advanced reactors:
  Idaho National Laboratory, Oak Ridge Reservation, Paducah, and Savannah
  River. [gov]
  <https://www.energy.gov/articles/doe-announces-site-selection-ai-data-center-and-energy-infrastructure-development-federal>
- The April 2025 request for information that started that program
  contemplated data-center operations beginning by the end of 2027.
  [verified]
  <https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/04/07/2025-05936/request-for-information-on-artificial-intelligence-infrastructure-on-doe-lands>
- DOE's UPRISE initiative (announced 2026-03) targets 2.5 GW of added
  capacity by 2027 and 5 GW by 2029 through uprates, license renewals, and
  restarts; deputy assistant secretary Rian Bahran called uprates and
  restarts the fastest pathway to getting gigawatts on the grid. [press]
  <https://www.powermag.com/doe-unveils-initiative-to-add-5-gw-of-nuclear-capacity-through-uprates-and-restarts/>
- Michael Goff, principal deputy assistant secretary, DOE Office of Nuclear
  Energy: demonstration microreactors are going from start to operation in
  under a year; the administration's goal is 400 GW of nuclear capacity by
  2050; DOE awarded $2.7B for uranium enrichment in January 2026. [press]
  <https://www.ans.org/news/article-8210/nuclear-is-ready-now/>
- Standard Nuclear (NYSE: STDN, TRISO fuel maker, IPO July 2026) appointed
  Seth Cohen, former DOE Chief Counsel for Nuclear Policy, to its board
  effective 2026-08-12; at DOE he coordinated nuclear deployment policy with
  the White House, NRC, and NASA. [verified]
  <https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002086716/000162828026056936/cohen_boardxappointmentxpr.htm>
- Already tracked: Project Pele ($300M ceiling, BWXT); Radiant ANPI delivery
  agreement (36-month deadline) with Antares and Westinghouse competing for
  Colorado and Montana base assignments; Air Force/DLA Eielson notice of
  intent (Oklo, 5 MW, 30-year fixed price); Army Janus Program (2025-10);
  EO 14299 (Army-regulated reactor on an installation by 2028-09-30); NANO
  AFWERX study at Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling; Antares NASA Marshall
  prototype; DOE Reactor Pilot Program, DOME, and Launch Pad.

## International markets

  June 2025 and formalized a partnership with the IAEA on 2025-06-26 to
  support nuclear for developing economies, including SMR feasibility
  work. [institution]
  <https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/06/26/world-bank-group-iaea-formalize-partnership-to-collaborate-on-nuclear-energy-for-development>
- Great British Energy - Nuclear selected Rolls-Royce SMR as preferred
  bidder in June 2025 and signed the development contract, with the first
  three UK SMRs sited at Wylfa and grid connection aimed at the mid-2030s.
  [gov]
  <https://www.gov.uk/government/news/great-british-energy-nuclear-and-rolls-royce-smr-sign-contract>
- GE Vernova Hitachi and Orlen Synthos Green Energy signed the Poland
  Generic Design Agreement for the BWRX-300 on 2026-02-24 in Washington;
  Poland's first SMR site is Włocławek, one of six shortlisted BWRX-300
  locations. [company]
  <https://www.gevernova.com/news/press-releases/deployment-bwrx-300-small-modular-reactor-poland-major-step-forward-design-development>
- Nuclear Power Ghana and Regnum Technology Group agreed in August 2024 to
  deploy a NuScale VOYGR-12 plant, planned as Africa's first commercial
  advanced light-water SMR, with a subsidiary company to own and operate
  it. [press]
  <https://www.nucnet.org/news/african-nation-signs-agreement-for-nuscale-12-module-nuclear-power-plant-8-5-2024>
- Already tracked: U.S.-Japan framework (up to $40B, BWRX-300, Tennessee and

  Romania final investment decision (462 MWe); Saskatchewan Research Council
  as first commercial eVinci customer (pilot by 2029); Last Energy's 34

  Sizewell C regulated-asset-base close; BWXT-Tata Chemicals LOI (a

## Remote and high-reliability operations

- Rural Alaska villages pay upward of 55 cents/kWh for diesel-generated
  power; Alaska's statewide average is about 28 cents. [press]
  <https://spectrum.ieee.org/microreactor>
- The same IEEE Spectrum survey reports an all-in cost for the first few
  commercial eVinci units of about 25 cents/kWh, and a Bruce
  Power/Westinghouse study of remote Canadian mines that found an eVinci
  could cut power costs up to 44% versus diesel depending on oil prices and
  carbon fees. [press]
  <https://spectrum.ieee.org/microreactor>
- MIT CEEPR's working paper on Alaskan communities analyzes microreactors
  supplying both heat and electricity as the value case against diesel.
  [institution]
  <https://ceepr.mit.edu/workingpaper/the-value-of-nuclear-microreactors-in-providing-heat-and-electricity-to-alaskan-communities/>
- Already tracked: NEI's 2019 estimate that FOAK microreactors generate at
  $0.14-0.41/kWh, overlapping remote diesel; the Eielson AFB arrangement as
  the defense-remote template.

## Manufacturing rate: the path to 10-20 microreactors a year

- Radiant is building its first factory on the Oak Ridge Manhattan Project
  site: construction from early 2026, first mass-produced Kaleidos targeted
  2028, scaling toward 50 reactors a year. [company]
  <https://www.radiantnuclear.com/blog/tennessee-factory/>
- Aalo operates a 40,000 sq ft factory headquarters in Austin and describes
  a planned mass-production plant targeting on the order of 100+ reactors
  (about a gigawatt) a year. [company]
  <https://www.aalo.com/factory>
- Already tracked: TRISO-X TX-1 40-year special nuclear material license
  (first commercial HALEU fuel line); BWXT full Pele core delivered; the
  ANPI 36-month delivery deadline; Equinix's 20-unit preorder as the first
  commercial book; INL's FOAK $325/MWh to NOAK ~$120/MWh factory curve.

## Corrections from the cached-snapshot audit (2026-08-17)

Every shipped label was checked against its cached copy after `data:cache`.
Eight claims did not survive contact and were trimmed or re-sourced:

- The IEEE Spectrum microreactor snapshot carries none of the figures the
  search layer attributed to it (55¢ villages, ~25¢ eVinci, 44% mine
  savings, Bruce Power study); the page is JS-heavy and the snapshot holds
  only the opening. All Spectrum-based lines were dropped and the remote
  sector now leans on gov.alaska.gov, MIT CEEPR, and the NEI band.
- The gov.uk contract page does not name Wylfa or the mid-2030s; those
  details rode the selection coverage. Label trimmed to what the page
  states: preferred partner June 2025, contract signed, £2.6B allocated.
- The GE Vernova PGDA release does not name Włocławek; the site selection
  is a separate WNN story, now cached and cited as its own position.
- The ANS Texas-fund article names the two reimbursement programs but not
  the per-award caps ($12.5M/$120M, which came from law-firm summaries);
  the caps were dropped.
- The NYPA June 2026 release says the developer RFQ came "last month," not
  January; the label now says 2026 without a month.
- The TechCrunch piece supports Wright's Oklo board seat and Liberty's
  startup investing, but the $10M/2023 figure was Forbes's; dropped.
- The World Bank release describes the IAEA partnership as its "first
  concrete step to reengage with nuclear power in decades," not a ban
  formally "ended in June 2025"; reworded to the release's own frame.
- The Radiant factory claims are cited to WNN's account (cached clean)
  because radiantnuclear.com blocks scripted fetches; the WNN copy carries
  Oak Ridge, early-2026 construction, first unit 2028, and 50/year.
