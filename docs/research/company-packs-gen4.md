As researched 2026-08-05.

Methodology note: "Binding" pipeline = an NRC construction permit has been issued AND the unit is under active construction AND it is tied to a named offtake system (regulated utility ratebase, or a specific funded deployment). "Non-binding" = LOIs, MOUs, master/framework agreements, IRP planning selections, and pipeline totals not yet backed by an issued construction permit or an executed, priced offtake contract. Under this test, Oklo's Aurora-INL (COL accepted for review, not issued) and all of X-energy's projects (no CP issued yet) count as non-binding even though they are each company's most advanced project — flagged so the test can be relaxed if Deployment Core's own methodology differs.
