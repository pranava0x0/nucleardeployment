## Aalo Atomics

- Design: Aalo-X is a full-scale, sodium-cooled "eXtra Modular Reactor" (XMR) rated at 30 MWt / 10 MWe; the commercial product bundles multiple units into a 50 MWe "Aalo Pod" power plant for AI data centers. [company] <https://www.aalo.com/aalo-x>
- Physical progress: Aalo's Critical Test Reactor (CTR) achieved initial criticality at Idaho National Laboratory on 2026-07-04 at approximately 12:20 a.m. MT — the fourth (and last, ahead of the deadline) of the DOE Reactor Pilot Program criticalities under Executive Order 14301, and the first new reactor built at INL in 50 years. [gov] <https://www.energy.gov/articles/department-energy-celebrates-fourth-criticality-ahead-july-4th-goal>
- Physical progress: the CTR went from groundbreaking to sustained chain reaction in under eight months; Aalo took on the largest scope of any Reactor Pilot Program participant, building, licensing, and operating the reactor facility itself and producing its own fuel assemblies. [press] <https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/microsoft-backed-smr-firm-aalo-achieves-test-reactor-criticality-first-under-doe-pilot-program/>
- Physical progress: Aalo-X's internal construction target (per Aalo's own update) was to begin construction in April 2026, sited on a previously-disturbed parcel inside the INL boundary formally allocated by INL's Land Use Committee; no confirmed criticality date found for Aalo-X itself as of 2026-08-05 ("not found 2026-08-05" — distinct from the CTR, which has already gone critical). [company] <https://www.aalo.com/post/aalo-x-progress-update-whats-next>
